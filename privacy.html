Alfred Global AI: Privacy Policy

Updated October 9, 2026

Alfred Global AI ("Alfred") is a personal assistant app published by Felix Villa. This policy explains what the app does with information.

Who the app is for

Alfred is not a public service. It only works when connected to a private server operated by the account holder. Without that connection, it runs in a demo mode with sample data.

What we collect

The developer does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information through this app. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no data is shared with data brokers.

How the app uses information

  • Messages, voice and photos: what you type, say or attach is sent only to the private server you connect the app to, so your assistant can answer.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: used for dictation, hands-free conversation and meeting recording when you start them.
  • Location: used on your device only, to tell whether you are at home or at work. It is not sent anywhere.
  • Contacts: used only when you choose to save or open a contact in your address book.
  • Photo library: used only for photos you pick, or to save an image you choose.
  • Face ID: used to unlock the app. Face ID data never leaves Apple's secure system and is never seen by the app.
  • Notifications: your device's push token is sent to your private server so it can send you notifications.

Services your server may use

To write replies, the private server you connect to sends your messages, voice transcripts and attachments to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, under that server owner's own account and Anthropic's terms. The app tells you this and asks you to agree before your first use. The developer does not receive that data.

Children

Alfred is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
